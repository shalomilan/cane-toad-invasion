How to read it

  • "Occupied" means "has toad records". Survey effort grew about 15-fold after 2017 (FrogID), so many cells behind the front got their first record late. That is why the model underestimates the number of new cells while placing the front well.
  • The slowdown at the Pilbara is an extrapolation. The toad has never met that desert, so no data can validate it yet. The two scenarios bracket how much we don't know about its climatic limits.
  • The western front is the third westernmost occupied cell west of 141°E; its advance is measured from its position in the first year with at least three occupied cells west of 141°E.
  • Records for 2025 are probably incomplete (upload lag in the Atlas of Living Australia).

Data: Atlas of Living Australia, GBIF, WorldClim 2.1, HydroRIVERS. Model: discrete-time survival (logistic regression) on a cell × year table, simulated 50 (backtest) and 200 (forecast) times. Code and notebooks.
